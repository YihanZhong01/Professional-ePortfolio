## About me

## Research interests

- **Causal inference:** confounding control for populations excluded from RCTs
- **Prediction models:** risk-of-bias evaluation and reliability in vulnerable groups
- **Chronic disease:** atrial fibrillation, anticoagulation, and cardiovascular outcomes
